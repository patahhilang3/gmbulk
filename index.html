How the Gmail Dot Trick works:

Gmail ignores dots (.) in the username part of the email address. For example, "your.name@gmail.com" and "yourname@gmail.com" are the same. This tool generates variations by adding dots in different positions, which can be useful for creating multiple accounts on websites while still receiving emails at your main Gmail address.
